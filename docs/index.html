The safe harbor

The rule that lets you sleep. Read it once, use it forever.

The IRS charges an underpayment penalty when you pay too little, too late. But there is a harbor: pay 100% of last year's total tax in four equal, on-time quarterly payments — 110% if last year's AGI topped $150k — and you owe zero penalty, even if this year's actual bill comes in higher. The harbor cares about last year's number, not this year's.
